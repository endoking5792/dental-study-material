# Preface

Preface
T
extbook of Conservative and Restorative Dentistry is
an updated version of the present concepts of
conservative and restorative dentistry. This book was
conceived based on valuable suggestions of my fellow
teachers and students all over the world, coupled with
newer developments in the field of restorative
dentistry. The book is expanded in 34 chapters covering
syllabi of all the universities. The recent concepts have
been highlighted in all the chapters, especially chapters
on Dentin Hybridization (Bonding), Composites,
Lasers, Diagnosis and Diagnostic Aids and Minimal
Invasive Dentistry, keeping in mind their clinical
significance in day-to-day practice. The language is
kept lucid along with self-explanatory photographs
and diagrams. The text is reinforced with clinical
photographs for easy understanding of the subject. The
keynotes are presented in box format for easy access
and understanding. I hope ‘Textbook of Conservative and
Restorative Dentistry’ is in a ‘must-read’ category for
the students, teachers and the practitioners as well,
pursuing restorative dentistry as a career.
Completing book of this magnitude would not
have been possible without contributions from
eminent academicians, multitude of teachers, students
and friend as well. I recognize their ‘contribution’ with
sincere thanks. I am indebted to my elder brother,
Mr RK Sikri, former Director, NTPC, for re-writing
text on ‘Forces Exerted on Teeth’, along with
modifying the line diagrams.
I am thankful to Dr Mohan Kumar (Chennai) and
Dr Vivek (Pune) for sharing their expertise in the field
of conservative dentistry. I am grateful to my
colleagues, Dr Renu Sroa, Principal, Dr Baljit Sidhu,
Associate Professor, Dr Jagan and Dr Sangeeta,
Medical officers for their constant help and
motivation. I am also thankful to my students,
Dr Akanksha, Dr Vikram, Dr Rajinder and Dr Swapnil
for checking and rechecking the manuscript.
I acknowledge the understanding and patience
of my gracious wife, Dr Poonam; my dear sons,
Dr Ankit, Dr Arpit, and my sweet daughters,
Dr Annupriya and Dr Jyotsana.
I request all the students and teachers to go
through the present edition and suggest areas of
improvement. Your comments and criticism will be
incorporated in future editions.
Vimal K Sikri
