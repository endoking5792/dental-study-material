# Foreword

Foreword
W
e are in the midst of rapidly advancing technical
developments in all areas of our profession. The
vast experience of Dr Sikri, coupled with his aptitude
for writing, conceived a nice way of presenting his
vision in the form of a book.
The book ‘Textbook of Conservative and Restorative
Dentistry’ comprehensively deals with clinical and
theoretical needs of all types of restorations. The book
shows the inherent vision of the author. Author’s
emphasis on originality and the language of the
subject matter is clearly evident in the book.
The current concepts of occlusion, composites and
minimal invasive dentistry are excellently presented;
the subject matter in other chapters also emphasizes
clinical aspects in day-to-day practice. It is hoped that
students, teachers and the practitioners will find an
accurate presentation of the subject and get benefited
from the same.
It is advisable to consider Dr Sikri’s book for
application in our daily teaching and clinical practice.
I hope this book will enlighten and encourage
exploring new horizons in the field of Restorative
dentistry.
With my profound faith and professional respect
for Dr Sikri, who is a popular teacher having vast
experience in the subject, I wish him excel in every
field, especially academics and research. I hope this
book would become treasure of knowledge amongst
students and teachers as well.
Dr. Zora Singh
Chancellor
Desh Bhagat University
