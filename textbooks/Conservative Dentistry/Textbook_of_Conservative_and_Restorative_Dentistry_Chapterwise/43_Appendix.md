• To change kilograms per square centimeter (kg/cm2) to pounds per square inch (lb/in2), multiply by

(1 kg/cm2 = 0.0981 MPa). Note 1 MN/m2 = 1 MPa.

0.070307 (1 lb/in = 0.0703 kg/cm2).

1000, = 1.273 kg/cm2 = 18.22 lb/in 2.
