Although fluorides inhibit mineral loss dur-
ing acid demineralization and enhance
remineralization,7 the therapeutic effect of
fluorides in high-risk situations is limited.11
Besides a preventive program focusing on
oral hygiene measures and decreasing the
frequency of sugar intake, 16 two options
are discussed for arresting initial lesions
and preventing progression:
1. Reduction or elimination of pathogenes
by means of antibacterial varnishes or
gels
2. Increasing host resistance against acid
attack by means of sealing root surfaces
with adhesive

has a low concentration of chlorhexidine
with 1 % thymol (to improve its substantivi-
ty). It showed promising results in labora-

the defect, there is no need to extend
preparation into sound dentin. Restoring
and sealing can be combined, thereby lim-
iting cavity preparation to the central caries
defect, followed by sealing of the sur-
rounding initial defect. Quite often this is
the only way to manage circumferential
root caries.
