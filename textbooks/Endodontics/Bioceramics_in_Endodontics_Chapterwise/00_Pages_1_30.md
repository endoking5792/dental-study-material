Clinicians today are challenged by rapidly evolving information related to clinical techniques and materials, making the process of critically appraising the available dental literature for focused, evidence-­based, patient-­centered care challenging.
Dr. Viresh Chopra has yet again put together a reference textbook, further assisting clinicians interested in practicing contemporary endodontics to make informed decisions when using “Bioceramics in Clinical Endodontics.”
“Bioceramics in Clinical Endodontics” provides the reader with not only the information required to have a sound understanding of the subject within today’s practice of clinical endodontics. This book also includes rich illustrations and numerous clinical cases along with an accessible video clip, making it clinically focused.
