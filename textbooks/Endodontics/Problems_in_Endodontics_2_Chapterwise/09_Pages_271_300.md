## Damage to clothing

## Nature of the problems

Minor chemical burns on patients' clothing are perhaps themost common form of accident during root canal irrigation; these burns are caused by drops of irrigation fluid or by the fog of spray produced when using an ultrasound system. Damage to clothes caused by sodium hypochlorite contamination is irreversible (the material is bleached) (Fig. 12-12).

## Prevention

These accidents can be prevented by covering clothing close to the patient's head with

a drape or bib, by careful suction and by screening the irrigation needle with one hand when removing a syringe from the oral caviy.

## Management

Any damage that is caused should be reported to the medico-legal insurance agent without delay, in agreement with the patient and with photographic documentation if possible.
