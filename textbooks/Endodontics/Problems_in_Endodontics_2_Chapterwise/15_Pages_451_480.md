## History and findings

The entire history of the tooth should be taken into consideration when planning a retreatment. Follow up of the recovery process is particularly important in this respect.
If the radiograph taken immediately following root canal obturation does not show any signs of an apical lesfon, and if such a lesion has only developed afterwards, then it is safe to assume that a post-treatment infection has developed and that retreatment wili be needed. However, if the radiograph already shows an apical lesion at the time ofthe rootcanal obturatton, itshould be borne In mind thatapical processes may take several years to heal and that an infection that may be persistent cannot be detected on the basis of radiological and clinical criteria alone.lD Consequently, the pertapical lesion that is still detectable in the radiograph may have entered a regression phase since the placement of the root canal obturation, and waiting may be a sensible approach (1-4 years; see Chapters 3 and 20).
Regardless of this, the onset of ciinical symptoms (tenderness, sinus tract, etc.) represents an indication for a retreatment or further treatment (extraction, apicectomy, etc.) even if the radiological apical lesion has become smaller. Progressive apl-

cal resorptions and alterations in neighboring structures (e.g. in the floor of the max-
~llary sinus) are also clear signs of the presence of pathological processes.
To date, the recommendation has been to consider each root separately when assessing the indications for a retreatment. But, since the different root canal systems in one tooth may intercommunicate, it is not likely that infection wili be restricted to only one root. The retreatment of "one root", with the aim of treating the apical perjodontitis detectable oniy around this root, is therefore not indicated.
This is particutarly true because the absence of a lesion does not necessarily indicate the absence of an infection. Consequently, a failed root canal treatment generally means that retreatment of the entire canal system will be necessary.
