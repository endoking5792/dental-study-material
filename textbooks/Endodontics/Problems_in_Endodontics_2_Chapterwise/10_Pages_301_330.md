## Blockage

Dentin chips or foreign bodies that get into the root canal during preparation (eg pulp stones, obturation material) can block the apical region of the root canai.

### Prevention

Frequent irrigation throughout the preparatron reduces the risk of artificial blockage of the root canai, as does the use of a patency file (a very fine root canal instrument, with a diameter of 0.1 rnm or less atthe tip, which is guided passively up to the end of
'I the root canal), or frequent recapitulation to the working length using a fine instrument.

### Management

If a blockage does occur, ultrasonically or sonicaily activated irrigation may be helpful.
Recapitulation can often be used to clear any blockage that is present. Apical prebending of any fine files that are used often facilitates the clearing process, especially when the foreign body is larger and can only be bypassed laterally.
